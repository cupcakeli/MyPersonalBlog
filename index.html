Welcome to my little corner of the internet

Hello, I'm Eliza

I'm Eliza Celine S. Fugaban, a first-year Information Technology student at Quezon City University. I'm at the beginning of a journey filled with new knowledge, challenges, and opportunities to discover what technology can do.

I'm especially interested in cybersecurity. I want to understand how networks, operating systems, and applications work, as well as how they can be protected from digital threats. I'm fascinated by ethical hacking, information security, and digital forensics.

My goal is to become a knowledgeable and responsible IT professional who can help protect people's information, investigate security incidents, and make digital spaces safer. I hope to continue improving my programming, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills along the way.

Outside my studies, I'm someone who finds happiness in books, music, coffee, baking, and the quiet beauty of everyday life. This website is where I collect a few of my favorite things and share little pieces of who I am.

My Hobbies

Journaling

Journaling is my personal space for thoughts that are sometimes difficult to say out loud. I enjoy writing about my days, recording memories, and reflecting on my growth. It helps me organize my thoughts and appreciate how much I have learned about myself over time.

Listening to Music

Music makes ordinary moments feel meaningful. I love how melodies and lyrics can express emotions that are hard to explain. Whether I am studying, relaxing, or simply daydreaming, music gives me comfort and makes every mood feel a little less lonely.

Baking

Baking is a comforting way for me to express creativity. I enjoy following recipes, measuring ingredients, and watching everything come together. It teaches me patience and attention to detail, and I love being able to share something homemade with other people.

Playing Games

Gaming gives me a chance to explore different worlds, experience stories, and solve interesting challenges. I enjoy the creativity and strategy involved in games. They also remind me that curiosity and experimentation are important, both in gaming and in learning technology.

Reading

Reading allows me to step into different worlds and understand perspectives beyond my own. I enjoy stories that leave me thinking long after I finish them, especially those that explore human nature, loneliness, identity, and the meaning of life. Books make quiet moments feel full.

My Blog

The Coffee I Keep Coming Back To

A little entry from Eliza's journal

Some people have a routine; I have a cup of coffee. It is the little ritual that helps me begin my day or settle into a long evening of studying. There is something comforting about holding a warm drink while the world outside feels busy and unpredictable.

As a student, I spend plenty of time learning new concepts, working on activities, and thinking about my future. Sometimes, a simple coffee break is enough to help me slow down and gather my thoughts before starting again.

I think these ordinary moments deserve to be remembered, too. Not every meaningful memory has to be an extraordinary event. Sometimes, happiness is just a good cup of coffee, a favorite song, and a little time to myself.

My Favorite Things

The songs, places, stories, and little comforts that feel like me.

Favorite Song

Strangers by Ethel Cain

I love the haunting atmosphere and emotional storytelling of this song. Its sound stays with me, making it the kind of music I can return to when I want something reflective and deeply atmospheric.

Favorite Drink

Coffee

Coffee is one of my favorite everyday comforts. I love the aroma, the quiet ritual of making a cup, and the feeling of having a little moment to myself before returning to the day.

Favorite Artist

Lana Del Rey

I appreciate her cinematic sound, nostalgic atmosphere, and distinctive storytelling. Her music often feels like a scene from an old film, full of wistfulness, beauty, and complicated emotions.

Favorite City

Edinburgh, Scotland

Edinburgh fascinates me with its historic architecture, old streets, literary associations, and atmospheric scenery. It is the kind of place I imagine exploring slowly, with a book and a warm drink in hand.

Favorite Season

Fall

I love the golden leaves, cool weather, cozy clothes, and peaceful atmosphere associated with autumn. Everything feels a little more nostalgic during this season, making it perfect for reading, journaling, and enjoying coffee.

Favorite Movie

Everything Everywhere All at Once

I love how the movie combines imaginative storytelling with questions about identity, family, choices, and meaning. Beneath all its unusual worlds, it carries a heartfelt reminder about kindness and connection.

Favorite Author

Franz Kafka

Kafka's writing interests me because of its unsettling atmosphere and exploration of alienation, identity, anxiety, and the absurdity of existence. His stories invite readers to think beyond the surface and question the world around them.
